Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how DEpthView handles information when you use this application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google, this application may receive basic information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address, and profile information, depending on the permissions provided by Google.

The application uses Google authentication to identify authorized users and provide access to the application's Google Sheets data.

2. Google Sheets Data

This application requests read-only access to a specific Google Spreadsheet using the Google Sheets API.

The requested permission is limited to:

Read-only access to Google Sheets data required for the 3D data visualization.

The application does not modify, delete, or write data to your Google Spreadsheet.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through Google authentication is used only to provide access to the application's functionality.

Spreadsheet information is used to generate the interactive 3D visualization displayed within the application.

4. Data Storage

This application does not maintain a separate database containing your Google account information.

Google authentication and authorization are handled through Google's authentication services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share your personal information with third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

This application uses third-party services including Google Identity Services, Google Sheets API, and Three.js for authentication, data access, and 3D visualization.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to ensure that authentication and data access are handled through Google's standard security mechanisms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the application owner.
